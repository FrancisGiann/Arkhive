# Arkhive modifications to Mihon

Historical source comments refer to the ReadingApp prototype. This published 0.4.5 snapshot retains Mihon's native source/image-reader foundation and adds Arkhive branding, cover-led library/navigation, local EPUB/PDF/text and online-novel reading, per-title reading preferences, controlled auto-read, exact-origin extension review, catalog discovery and local library/offline tools.

Mihon v0.21.1 upstream commit: `e9670d8a12ff4526d9ca09daa73280b8c42b9bb0`. Upstream copyright and Apache-2.0 notices remain intact. Readium BSD, Noto SIL OFL and LNReader/helper MIT/ISC notices are retained alongside the shipped assets. Source/reader scripts retain their trust, sanitization, HTTPS and explicit confirmation boundaries. No DRM/paywall bypass is provided.

See [README.md](README.md) for build requirements and the repository's root README/release notes for current validation and limitations. Local signing keys, caches, user data and private QA records are excluded.
